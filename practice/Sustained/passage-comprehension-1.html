On Sunday morning, the Mehta family left their home at 8:15 a.m. for a picnic at Lakeview Park. Before leaving, they packed 3 lunch boxes, 2 water coolers, 1 first-aid kit, and a basket of fruit. They reached the park at 9:05 a.m. and spent the first hour near the boating area before moving to the shaded lawn for lunch. At 11:20 a.m., the youngest child counted 18 ducks near the pond, and by noon the family had taken 27 photographs. Later, while reading a newspaper report, they noticed that India’s Census exercise had recorded data across 28 states and 8 union territories. The article also mentioned that a city survey team visited 240 households in one ward and completed the count in 6 days. The family returned home at 4:10 p.m. after a long but pleasant outing.
