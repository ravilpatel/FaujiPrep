Power of Expression
Basic Understanding

Communicating ideas clearly and confidently so others understand quickly.

Meaning

Clear and confident communication

Speak with clarity, structure, and calm tone.

In Practice

Give precise instructions

People understand you the first time.

Example Scenario

Short team briefing

You explain the plan in 60 seconds and everyone is clear.

What It Shows

Message clarity

You communicate without confusion or repetition.

Build It

Practice short briefings

Summarize topics in 60 seconds and get feedback.

Self Check

Do people ask you to repeat?

If they do, simplify your structure and wording.
