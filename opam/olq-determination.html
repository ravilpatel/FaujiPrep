OLQ Guide

Determination
Basic Understanding

Persisting until the task is complete, even when obstacles appear.

Meaning

Finish what you start

Persist until the goal is done.

In Practice

Push through setbacks

You keep moving when progress is slow.

Example Scenario

Repeated failure in a task

You adjust approach and complete the task anyway.

What It Shows

Persistence under pressure

You do not quit when it gets uncomfortable.

Build It

Set small goals daily

Track progress and finish what you promise.

Self Check

Do you quit too early?

You stay on task until it is complete.
