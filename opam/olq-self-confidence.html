OLQ Guide

Self Confidence
Basic Understanding

Trusting your ability to act and decide without panic or arrogance.

Meaning

Calm belief in yourself

Steady confidence without arrogance.

In Practice

Commit to decisions

You decide and follow through with control.

Example Scenario

Time pressure in a task

You choose a plan and stick with it calmly.

What It Shows

Steady composure

You do not panic or waver.

Build It

Prepare and rehearse

Practice skills and review successes.

Self Check

Do you stay steady under scrutiny?

You hold your ground calmly.
