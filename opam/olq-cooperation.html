OLQ Guide

Cooperation
Basic Understanding

Working smoothly with others and supporting team goals over personal credit.

Meaning

Be a reliable teammate

Work smoothly, respect roles, and share effort.

In Practice

Support the team

You help others and keep the group moving.

Example Scenario

Group obstacle with uneven skills

You take a role, assist a weaker member, and finish together.

What It Shows

Group-first mindset

You put team success above personal credit.

Build It

Work in group tasks

Volunteer, listen actively, and deliver on time.

Self Check

Do people enjoy working with you?

Teammates want to work with you again.
