OLQ Guide

Stamina
Basic Understanding

Maintaining physical and mental endurance over long periods.

Meaning

Endurance with focus

Sustain effort over time.

In Practice

Stay consistent in long tasks

You keep energy steady late.

Example Scenario

Long activity with fatigue

You maintain pace while others slow down.

What It Shows

Consistent output

Your performance stays steady to the end.

Build It

Train body and mind

Fitness, routines, and disciplined rest help.

Self Check

Do you fade late?

Your output stays strong in the final hour.
