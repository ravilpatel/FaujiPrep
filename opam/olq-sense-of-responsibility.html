OLQ Guide

Sense of Responsibility
Basic Understanding

Owning duties and being accountable for results, good or bad.

Meaning

Own the outcome

Accept duties and finish what you take on.

In Practice

Be reliable under pressure

You report honestly and fix mistakes.

Example Scenario

A task slips behind

You admit the delay and correct it without blaming.

What It Shows

Accountability

You take ownership of results.

Build It

Take ownership daily

Finish tasks and follow up without reminders.

Self Check

Do others rely on you?

People trust you with important work.
