OLQ Guide

Initiative
Basic Understanding

Taking action without waiting for instructions when action is needed.

Meaning

Start without being told

You act when action is needed.

In Practice

Act in uncertain moments

You move first with a responsible plan.

Example Scenario

No roles assigned in a task

You step up, assign roles, and start the work.

What It Shows

Responsible first mover

You do not wait for instructions to contribute.

Build It

Volunteer and lead small tasks

Find gaps, start action, report progress.

Self Check

Do you act when others pause?

You step up instead of waiting.
