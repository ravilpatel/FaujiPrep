🚀 PHASE 1 — Do These First

Covers ~60–65 Questions. This should form the core of your preparation.

1️⃣ Arithmetic

~22–25 Questions • 30% Study Time

Highest-scoring chapter. Every sub-topic below has appeared every year.

📌 Percentage / Profit & Loss / Discount 5–6 Qs

  • Profit & Loss calculations
  • Successive Discount
  • Marked Price vs Selling Price
  • Percentage Change / Increase / Decrease
  • Population Growth problems
  • Price Reduction & Consumption problems

📌 Time, Speed & Distance 3–4 Qs

  • Train problems (length, crossing)
  • Boat & Stream (upstream / downstream)
  • Relative Speed
  • Average Speed
  • Circular Track meeting problems

📌 Time & Work 2–3 Qs

  • Men-days-work formula
  • Pipe & Cistern
  • Alternate day work pattern
  • Efficiency comparison

📌 Ratio, Proportion & Partnership 2–3 Qs

  • Ratios & simplification
  • Partnership profit distribution
  • Proportion (Direct & Inverse variation)

📌 Simple Interest & Compound Interest 2 Qs

  • CI vs SI difference formula
  • Compound growth over periods
  • Quarterly/Half-yearly compounding

📌 Average / Mixture / Alligation 2 Qs

  • Average with replacement
  • Mixture concentration
  • Weighted average

📌 Age Problems 1–2 Qs

  • Present age setup
  • Ratio-based age problems
  • Future/Past age conditions

2️⃣ Algebra

~18–22 Questions • 25% Study Time

Second highest chapter. Identities and polynomials dominate.

📌 Algebraic Identities 4 Qs

  • Standard expansion identities: (a+b)², (a–b)², (a+b)³, (a³+b³)
  • Simplification using identities
  • Surds simplification

📌 Polynomial & Factorization 5 Qs

  • Factor Theorem
  • Polynomial Remainder Theorem
  • Polynomial Division
  • HCF & LCM of polynomials
  • Relation between roots and coefficients

📌 Quadratic Equations 3–4 Qs

  • Sum & Product of roots
  • Nature of roots (discriminant)
  • Equation formation from given roots

📌 Logarithms 2–3 Qs

  • log identities (product, quotient, power rules)
  • Exponent ↔ log conversion
  • log base 10 calculation

📌 Indices & Exponents 2 Qs

  • Unit digit of powers
  • Power simplification
  • Laws of indices

📌 Linear Equations 2 Qs

  • Simultaneous equations (2 variables)
  • Analytical & graphical solutions
  • Inequality-based problems

3️⃣ Number System

~8–9 Questions • 10% Study Time

High consistency across all papers. Easy to score.

📌 Topics

  • HCF & LCM (word problems)
  • Divisibility rules (2, 3, 4, 5, 9, 11)
  • Prime & Composite numbers
  • Remainder Theorem
  • Cyclic Remainder (unit digit patterns)
  • Unit digit of expressions
  • Consecutive numbers properties
  • Rational vs Irrational classification
  • Euclidean Algorithm

⚙️ PHASE 2 — Do These Next

Covers ~20 More Questions. Builds upon the foundational chapters.

4️⃣ Mensuration

~8–10 Questions • 10% Study Time

Extremely scoring. CDS I 2026 showed strong 3D dominance.

📌 2D Mensuration 4 Qs

  • Rectangle & Square (area, perimeter)
  • Rhombus
  • Triangle (area using base-height / Heron's)
  • Circle (area, circumference, sector, segment)
  • Surface-perimeter relation problems

📌 3D Mensuration 5–6 Qs ⚠️ Trending Up

  • Cylinder (CSA, TSA, Volume)
  • Cone (slant height, CSA, Volume)
  • Sphere & Hemisphere
  • Cuboid & Cube
  • Water displacement / overflow problems
  • Volume comparison problems

5️⃣ Trigonometry

~8–10 Questions • 10% Study Time

📌 Trigonometric Identities 4 Qs

  • sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 and derived forms
  • tan–sec relation: sec²θ – tan²θ = 1
  • cosec–cot relation
  • Identity verification & simplification

📌 Standard Values 2 Qs

  • Values at 0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°
  • Table-based direct value questions

📌 Heights & Distances 2–3 Qs

  • Tower height problems
  • Pole & shadow problems
  • Angle of elevation & depression
  • Two-observer problems

  • Inscribed Angle Theorem
  • Chord properties

📌 Quadrilaterals 2 Qs

  • Cyclic Quadrilateral
  • Parallelogram properties
  • Rectangle & Square properties

📌 Lines & Angles 1 Q

  • Parallel lines, transversals
  • Basic angle properties

💡 Quick Strategy Tips

  • Never guess blindly — Negative marking is –0.33. Skip if unsure.
  • Attempt Arithmetic first in the exam — highest volume, moderate difficulty.
  • 3D Mensuration is trending up (especially CDS I 2026) — don't skip it.
  • Identities in Algebra are direct formula-based — fastest marks.
  • Statistics is least rewarding — invest here only after Phase 1 & 2 are solid.
  • Speed matters — 100 questions in 120 minutes = 1.2 min/question.
